About Hey Kay Budgets

Published by Kay | Updated October 15, 2026

Practical Budgeting Help

Hey Kay Budgets publishes beginner-friendly guides, free calculators, and printable worksheets for planning paychecks, saving for irregular expenses, and understanding debt payments.

Kay is the name used in the site bylines. For questions about an article, use the Contact page.

Editorial Approach

Worked budgets and household scenarios are illustrations unless clearly identified otherwise. They are not records of Kay's finances, client results, or promises of what a reader will achieve.

Guides explain assumptions, show the arithmetic, link related concepts, and distinguish checking-account buffers, one-month-ahead reserves, sinking funds, and emergency savings.

If you find incorrect math, unclear advice, or a broken tool, report a correction with the page address and the issue. Dates change when substantive content changes.

Using the Tools

The debt payoff calculator estimates each debt independently using fixed monthly payments and a constant rate. The mortgage calculator models principal, interest, entered escrow, and fees. Actual lender calculations can differ.

The free resources hub offers printable worksheets without an email signup. The Privacy Policy explains calculator processing, optional analytics, and advertising scripts.
